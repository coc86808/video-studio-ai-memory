The baker said: Allah answered all my prayers, except one final wish.
Centuries ago, the great scholar Imam Ahmad was traveling through a distant city.
Exhausted by the freezing rain, he sought shelter inside a mosque.
But the guard pushed him out into the cold dark street.
Across the road, a humble baker saw the shivering stranger and invited him inside.
As he kneaded the dough through the quiet midnight hours, the baker continuously whispered:
Astaghfirullah. Astaghfirullah.
Imam Ahmad asked: What fruit have you seen from this constant repentance?
The baker smiled: Allah answered every prayer I ever made. Except one: to meet Imam Ahmad before I die.
Imam Ahmad began to weep and said:
SubhanAllah. Allah not only answered your prayer, but He dragged Ahmad across Iraq and threw him at your doorstep.
The Prophet taught: Whoever is constant in Istighfar, Allah grants relief from every distress and provides from where he never expects.
