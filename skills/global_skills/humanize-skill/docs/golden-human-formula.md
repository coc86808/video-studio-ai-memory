
## 5. Golden Formula for 100% Human Score (Zero-Detector Detection)
To produce text that passes AI detectors with 0% AI detection:
1. **Direct Coordinate Action**: Use direct simple past/present declarative sentences ('We put fifty saplings...', 'My brother and I wrapped...').
2. **Zero Nested Subordinate Chains**: Ban nested conditionals ('If you want X to Y, you just need Z so that W'). Replace with sequential actions.
3. **Hyper-Specific Human Constraints & Math**: Use exact real numbers ('fifty saplings', 'twenty-five survived', 'two buckets'), physical landmarks ('tube-well', 'tin fence', 'ditch'), and specific schedules ('Tuesday and Friday morning').
4. **Blunt Factual Punches**: Insert 4-8 word direct statements between narrative sentences.
5. **No Clichés or Abstract Outcomes**: End with a concrete visual marker ('branches are taller than the tin fence') rather than a philosophical summary.
